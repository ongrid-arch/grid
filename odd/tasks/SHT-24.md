# Sheet claims and access notes (SHT-24)

## Outcome

A client sheet refuses a headline or introduction that claims approval, compliance or
accessibility. The cover shows measurements under the person's access figures even when the
introduction runs onto another page.

## Design

Build on the sheet's existing claim check and refusal. Check a headline or paragraph wrapped
in quotation marks as a claim in its own right. Keep the existing treatment of quotes inside
prose. Normalize spacing and hyphens for matching phrases while keeping the author's print
text as given.

Put access measurements first among the cover notes and lay out the notes before the
introduction. Keep the existing limit and pointer to the full measurement list.

## Done

Check the tool's refusal for supplied and saved text. Read the first page of the PDF back to
find the headline, door shortfalls and turning-space shortfall with a long introduction.
Run the build, type check, lint, affected tests and scoreboard.
