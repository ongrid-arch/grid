# Roof overhangs in elevation

## Outcome

A pitched roof reads as a roof in elevation. Its covering sits above the walls and the eaves and verges project past them. The visible ridge, hips, and gutters follow the roof.

## Design

Build on the elevation's existing roof planes, courses, and edge marks. Add a nominal drawing projection at the exposed roof perimeter and carry the same planes out over it. Keep the cuts where the roof meets upper floors. The plan has no overhang dimension, so the projection belongs to the elevation and its silhouette.

## Done

Check that gable and hip roofs extend beyond the outside walls, that the slope and edge details meet the projected fascia, and that an abutment or a flat roof keeps its edge. Verify the SVG in the working and technical looks. Run the build, type check, lint, affected package suites, and scoreboard.
