# 2026-11-26: the plot, streets, set-backs and north written through tools and rooms text (CNT-41)

## For people

- The plot, its streets and set-backs, north and the site's trees, paths, pools, fences and gates no longer need the plan file edited by hand. Rooms text writes them in lines of their own (`plot 12 x 25`, `street south`, `setback street 5`, `setback all 2`, `north 135`, `north 30 clockwise`, `north up-left`, `site origin 0,0`, `site location 31.23,121.47`, `site tree oak at 3,4 canopy 5`, `site path drive 0,0 0,10 width 1.2`), `grid describe --as rooms` reads them back, and `grid write` keeps the parts of the site a text does not write. The plot's answer gives its area in acres too on a plan in feet and inches, and says which outdoor space the coverage counts (roofed porches and carports) and leaves out (open decks and terraces) (CNT-41).

## For agents

- New tool `grid_set_site`: sets the plot's `boundary` (corners as `"x,y"` text) or `plot_width` × `plot_depth` (at `plot_x`, `plot_y`, or centred on the building and said in `guessed`), `streets` (a side of the drawing, every edge facing it, or an edge number), `setbacks` (`"street 5"`, `"all 2"`, `"west 40 assumed"`, `"edge 3 1.5"`), `north` or `north_points`, `origin`, `latitude` and `longitude`, `elevation`, `ground` and `country`, and `remove` takes fields out. A new boundary keeps the streets and set-backs that still name one of its edges (the others in `couldNotKeep`). It answers as every change does, then with the plot as `grid_measure what=site` gives it, in `site`.
- New tool `grid_change_site`: `action` `add` (`kind`, `points` or `at`, `width`, `canopy`, `height`, `label`, `id`), `move` (`id`, `by` or `to`), `resize` (`id`, and what changes) or `remove` (`id`), one thing a call; says when a thing stands outside the plot's boundary, and answers with `thing` and `things`. The tool server lists 40 tools; the two come after `grid_tidy` and before `grid_save_view`.
- `grid_measure what=site`: with no boundary, the line names `grid_set_site` and the rooms text lines; every plot gets a line on what the coverage counts; `boundary.acres` is in the data. `what=facing` on a plan with no north names `grid_set_site` and the `north` line.
- Rooms text: the lines `plot`, `site`, `street`, `setback` and `north`; `rooms/site` refuses one it cannot read, with its line, and `rooms/plot-centred` says where a plot with no `at` was put. `raw site` is told the site has lines of its own. `grid_write_rooms` answers with the plot's line when the text writes any of the site. No new code in the record.
