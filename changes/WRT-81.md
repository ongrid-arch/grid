# 2026-11-27: existing, demolished and new in rooms text (WRT-81)

## For people

- Rooms text writes a renovation: `phase existing`, `phase demolished` or `phase new` on a room, door, window, opening, `place` or `repeat` line, or alone on a line for the lines after it (`phase existing` at the top marks a survey). Grid works out the walls before and after the work: a wall that stands in both is existing, one only before is to be demolished, one only after is new, and a door into a demolished room goes with it. `phase <word>` on a wall line marks that wall. Reading a plan with phases back as rooms text gives these words again instead of raw lines, and `grid write` takes that text over the plan (WRT-81).

## For agents

- `grid_write_rooms` and `grid write` take `phase <existing|demolished|new>` on room, door, window, opening, `place`, `repeat` and `wall` lines, and alone on a line as the phase of the room and opening lines after it. The text is written as the building before the work and after it and made one plan: walls and rooms in both states state `existing`, a part only before `demolished`, only after `new`; walls at the very same place are one existing wall under the id after the work; a part with one id and two shapes keeps its id after the work, new, and the one before is `<id>-before`, demolished; an opening in both states states only what its line says, so it goes with its wall. A problem of one state starts "Read as the building before the work" or "after the work". A phase word refused (an unknown phase, on a line that takes none, in a fit-out) is `rooms/phase`. `grid_describe as=rooms` writes `phase existing` at the top of a plan with phases and the phase words of each part that is not existing, and compares phases as the plan reads them. `grid write` over a plan with phases refuses (`rooms/phases`) only a text that states no phase, with new words: such a text, and `--replace`, writes every part with none.
