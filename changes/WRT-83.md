# 2026-11-27: structural grids, straight or radial, and columns set out on them in rooms text (WRT-83)

## For people

- Rooms text writes the plan's structural grids, `grid G1 x 4@7.5 y 3@6` (names, a turn and the floors it is drawn on optional) and radial ones, `grid P polar at 15,10 rings 5.9 9 rays 0 to 315 every 45`, drawn with their rays, rings as arcs or whole circles, names and ring spacing. Columns stand at every crossing (`columns c 0.4 on grid G1`), at one (`column at grid 7/B 0.4`) or evenly round a circle (`columns ring 8 radius 5.9 round 0.2`, round the radial grid when no centre is written). A column with no floor stands on the floor at level 0 instead of being left out. `module 1.2` sets a planning module, so rooms and points are written in modules (`5mod x 4mod`). Reading a plan back as rooms text writes its grids as grid lines (WRT-83).

## For agents

- `grid_write_rooms` and `grid write` take `grid <id> ["<name>"] [at x,y] [turn <deg>] x <spacing> ... [names ...] y <spacing> ... [names ...] [floors <floor> ...]` (`<n>@<s>` repeats a spacing), `grid <id> polar [at x,y] rings <radius> ... [names ...] rays <deg> ... [names ...]` (lists one by one, as `<n>@<s>` steps or `<a> to <b> every <s>`) and `raw grid {...}`, into the plan's `grids`; grid lines replace the file's grids, a text with none keeps them. `columns <group> <size> on grid <id>` (ids `<group>-<x>-<y>`), `column [<id>] <size> at grid [<grid>] <x>/<y>` (id `c-<x>-<y>` when none is written; either order) and `columns [<group>] ring <n> radius <r> [at x,y] [start <deg>] <size>` (ids `<group>-1` and on; group `ring` when none) set columns out; a grid may follow the columns on it. A column with no floor in a text whose floors include no `ground` stands on the level-0 floor (else the lowest). `module <length>` and `<n>mod` lengths. C25 gains `polar: { radius }` on a grid: `origin` its centre, `x` its rays (degrees from `turn`), `y` its rings (metres from `radius`); rays less than 360 degrees in all. `PlanGridLine` may carry `path`, `outTo` and `namedAt`, `PlanGrid` `polar`; new limit `gridArcStep` (5 degrees). Refusals use `rooms/grid`, `rooms/column`, `rooms/module`, `rooms/duplicate-id` and `rooms/unknown-floor`. No new code in the check record.
