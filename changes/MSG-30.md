# 2026-11-25: the label said once where no figure is applied (MSG-30)

## For people

- An agent no longer opens every answer about your plan with "General guidance, not your local
  rules" when the answer applies no figure from a code (reading the plan, a picture, a count, a
  comparison). The label stays on every check, measurement and report, and on every picture file.

## For agents

- `grid_describe`, `grid_view`, `grid_compare`, `grid_fields`, `grid_find` and `grid_count` no
  longer begin their text with the label; their data still carries `label`. Give the label once,
  where you first give a figure or a check, not at the head of every message. The instructions
  and the guide say so. No new code in the record.
