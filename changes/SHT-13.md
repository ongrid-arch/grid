# 2026-11-25: the "not shown" note lists only what is left out (SHT-13)

## For people

- The note under a drawing, and under each floor of the client sheet, no longer says furniture or
  structure is not shown when the floor draws furniture, columns or a structural grid. It lists
  only what that floor leaves out; services and landscape are always listed (SHT-13).

## For agents

- The sheet's note reads "Drawn from the plan alone. Not shown: ..." with the things that floor
  does not draw (furniture, stairs, structure, services, landscape), in the sheet's language. No
  new code in the record.
