# 2026-11-25: ground dug away in front of an opening is not below the ground (ARE-37)

## For people

- A door or window onto a light well, an area, a cutting or the foot of a ramp is no longer named
  as below the ground. If the light well you drew has its floor above the opening's foot, the note
  names that light well so you can lower it (ARE-37).

## For agents

- `opening/below-ground` leaves out an opening whose drawn outdoor room has its floor at or under
  the opening's foot, and a door at the foot of a ramp. Where the outdoor room's floor is above the
  foot, the finding stays and its wording names that room. No new code in the record.
