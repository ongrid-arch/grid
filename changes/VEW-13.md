# 2026-11-25: room labels stay inside their rooms (VEW-13)

## For people

- A room's label stays in its room on every picture, sheet and view. When the whole label does
  not fit, Grid turns it along a long narrow room, writes it smaller, wraps the name on two
  lines or leaves out the area, and last writes the room's number alone, its own number when
  the plan states one, with the numbers and names listed under "Rooms" in the band below the
  drawing ("1–24 Storage unit"). Only a room too small even for its number gets a leader, a
  short one that crosses no other room to a label outside the building. No label sits on a
  stair's flights or a lift's cross (VEW-13).

## For agents

- Pictures from `grid_view`, `grid_preview`, `grid draw` and `grid sheet` keep each room's label in its
  room: a label that does not fit whole is turned, smaller, wrapped or the room's number, and
  the picture's key band lists the numbered rooms under "Rooms". Labels no longer fly outside
  the building on long, crossing leaders, so a picture no longer names the wrong room. No new
  code in the record.
