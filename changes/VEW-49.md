# 2026-11-25: a doorless opening in elevation is a void with reveals (VEW-49)

## For people

- A doorless opening in an elevation is drawn as a void with its reveals, and what stands behind it, such as a recessed shop door, is seen through it instead of the entrance looking flush (VEW-49).

## For agents

- Each opening of a facade (`planFacade`) carries `reveal`, the thickness in metres of the wall it is cut in. A doorless opening lets the face behind it show, so a door on a deeper wall is kept. No new code in the record.
