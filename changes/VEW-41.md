# 2026-11-25: a stair seen beyond a section's cut is one profile (VEW-41)

## For people

- In a section, a stair or long run of steps lying beyond the cut is drawn as its sides and soffit and one face for its risers, no longer one outlined column per step, so a flight with dozens of risers no longer looks like a bar chart (VEW-41).

## For agents

- `grid_section` and `grid draw` sections: the faces beyond the cut of a flight give its two sides, its top end and one face for its risers seen from the foot, where each riser gave a face of its own. No new code in the record.
