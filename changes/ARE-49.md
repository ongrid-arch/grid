# 2026-11-25: no brief reminder on a survey (ARE-49)

## For people

- Writing a plan that records what stands (every part existing, taped lengths kept, or only measured or scanned marks) no longer asks for a brief (ARE-49).

## For agents

- `reminder` in the answer of `grid_write_rooms`, `grid write` and a scan is null for a survey: a plan whose every part is `existing`, that keeps `taped` lengths, or whose marks are all `measured` or `scanned`. No new code in the record.
