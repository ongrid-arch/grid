# 2026-11-26: notes, tags, symbols, routes and sketch lines written through a tool and rooms text (SHT-22)

## For people

- Writing on the drawing no longer needs the plan file edited by hand. Rooms text places notes, tags, symbols, the key and the sketch in lines of their own, by floor, in a room or at a point: `tag "You are here" at 3,4`, `tag "FE" in kitchen`, `symbol entrance at 2,-0.5 "Way out"`, `key`, `sketch shape garden 0,-1 8,-1 8,-6 0,-6`, and `route exit 2,5 2,1 6,1`, the way out drawn as a line with an arrowhead at its end. `on <floor>` names the floor (a line under a `floor` line is on that floor), `id <id>` names the entry. The drawing, the sheet and the DXF export show them as they show every note and sketch. `grid describe --as rooms` reads them back as these lines, and `grid write` keeps the file's when the text writes none (SHT-22).

## For agents

- New tool `grid_annotate`: one entry from `kind` (`note`, `tag`, `symbol`, `key`, `route`, `line`, `shape`, `rect`, `arc`, `text`) with `text`, `symbol`, `at` (`"x,y"`), `room`, `turn`, `points`, `category`, `radius`, `start`, `end` and `id`, or many at once in `lines`, each a rooms text line; `floor` for an entry that names none; `remove` takes entries out by id first. It answers as every change does, then with each entry placed (`<id>: <what> <where>, on floor <floor>`) and what each floor carries, and `placed`, `removed`, `annotations` and `sketch` (ids) in the data; an entry it cannot place refuses the call with nothing written, naming it (`lines[1]: ...`). The tool server lists 50 tools; it comes after `grid_place_trace` and before `grid_save_view`.
- Rooms text: the `note`, `tag`, `symbol`, `key`, `route` (or `arrow`) and `sketch line|shape|rect|arc|text` lines, and `raw note {...}` and `raw sketch {...}`; `raw` now takes `wall`, `room`, `opening`, `floor`, `stair`, `note` or `sketch`. `rooms/note` refuses a line Grid cannot place, with its line; two entries with one id are `rooms/duplicate-id`. The answer's guesses list what the lines placed, by floor. The rooms grammar has a `writing on the drawing` topic.
- A route is a sketch `line` whose last three points draw a V-shaped head at its last point (at most 0.4 m long); it is drawn and exported as every sketch line is, and `floor/sketch-drawn-only` names it as it names every sketch item. No new code in the record.
