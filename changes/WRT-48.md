# 2026-11-25: unknown or misplaced raw input in rooms text is refused by name (WRT-48)

## For people

- Rooms text no longer drops what it does not read. A `raw wall` with `bulge`, `spline` or `ellipse` is refused with how a curve is written (`"arc"`), where a straight wall used to be written; a raw room's `facts` are pointed to `"properties"`; a window's `inset` and any other field the plan file does not have are named with the fields that part has. A `raw floor` with no floor line above it now gives its roof or parapet to the ground floor, where it used to vanish. `raw door`, `raw stair`, `raw column`, `raw fixture` and `raw site` are told where that part goes. `plan north 0` is refused instead of naming the plan "north", and a wall line naming a room that does not exist is refused instead of doing nothing (WRT-48).

## For agents

- `grid_write_rooms` and `grid write`: a raw wall, room or opening with a field Grid's plan file does not have (on the part, or inside one of its fields, such as `"center"` in `"arc"`) is refused with its line (`rooms/raw`), the field, the nearest field Grid knows, and the fields that place has; a curve word on a raw wall is answered with the `arc` form, and a facts word with `properties`. A `raw floor` field other than `roof`, `parapet`, `outline`, `homes`, `parking` and `level` is refused the same way (`rooms/raw`), with the floor line for `height`, `elevation`, `slab`, `name` and `building`. A raw floor written with no floor line applies to the ground floor, and the plan then writes `floors` with that one floor. Any `raw <word>` other than `wall`, `room`, `opening` or `floor` (and `pattern`, refused as before) is refused with `rooms/raw` and where that part goes (`raw opening` for a door or window; the plan file's `stairs`, `columns`, `items`, `site`, `bays`, `ramps` or `grids`), where it was `rooms/syntax` with a list of line words. A `plan` line with words after its one name is refused (`rooms/syntax`) with the line written right. `wall between <a> and <b>` and `wall <room> <side>` naming no room of their floor once every line is read are refused (`rooms/unknown-room`) with the nearest room id and the floor's rooms. Each of these is one problem per field or name, all at once. No new code in the record.
