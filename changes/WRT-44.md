# 2026-11-25: every rooms read-back writes back, and a wall with no length never stops Grid (WRT-44)

## For people

- What `grid describe --as rooms` gives back, `grid write` takes, copied floors included. A pair of doors as wide as its corridor (6 ft on 6 ft) stays a door line on every copied floor instead of being refused; a plan drawn in feet with 4.5 in walls reads back with its walls where they are; a row of houses whose kitchens open to their living rooms reads back as rooms text; and a floor after one that comes in Grid's own terms keeps its ids. The writer no longer writes a wall with no length, so a row of flats whose bedroom wing is wider than its living wing is written instead of refused by its own check, and writing rooms text over a plan that holds such a wall no longer ends with Grid saying something went wrong inside it (WRT-44).

## For agents

- `grid_write_rooms` and `grid write`: a worked-out wall whose two ends are one point is left out of the plan (the walls beside it cover the place); the other walls keep their ids. An opening that lands in such a place is refused with its line (`rooms/opening-fit`), and a `raw wall` line whose start and end are one point is refused with its line (`rooms/raw`) where the writing used to throw. `grid write --replace` over a plan holding a wall with no length compares the plans without throwing.
- `grid_describe` and `grid describe --as rooms`: a plan holding a wall with no length reads back without it and without the openings in it, each named in a `# Left out: …` line at the top, and the reading lists them in `leftOut`; the `based on` line still names the file's revision. An opening that runs past its room's side by no more than two tenths of a millimetre is written to fit the side (`0 from north, 1.82875 wide`). Room places and sizes are written to a hundredth of a millimetre when the plan's walls fall there. A dividing line shared by more than two rooms is written `wall between <a> and <b> virtual` for each two rooms across it. A refusal with no line that names a part (an id given twice, a room the check cannot close) gives that part's floor raw, not the first floor still in rooms text. No new code in the record.
