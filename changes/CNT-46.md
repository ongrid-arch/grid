# 2026-11-26: parking bays and ramps written in rooms text and through tools (CNT-46)

## For people

- A car park no longer needs its bays and ramps written into the plan file by hand. Rooms text fills a room with rows of bays and the aisles between them (`bays b1 in deck fill, 2.5 x 5, aisle 6, numbered B1-01`), lays one row against a wall (`bays g in deck along north, accessible, count 4`), writes one bay (`bay p1 at 2,3, 2.5 x 5`), and a ramp between two floors, straight in its room (`ramp r1 from b1 to ground in deck, 4 wide, straight, runs east along north`), along its own middle line, or as a helix (`ramp h from b1 to ground in spiral, 3.5 wide, helix, turns 1`). `raw bay {...}` and `raw ramp {...}` take the plan file's own JSON. A kind word (`car`, `small`, `accessible`, `motorcycle`) gives Grid's own starting size, not a code's. The bays and ramps are the plan's own, so the drawing, the bay counts, the takeoff, the slab openings and the slope check show them. `grid describe --as rooms` writes them back as rows, helices and ramps in their rooms, and `grid write` keeps the file's bays and ramps when the text writes none, and says so (CNT-46).

## For agents

- New tool `grid_place_bays`: `group` and `room`, then the module (`width` and `length`, or `kind`), and `along` (one row against that side, with `count`, `offset`, `inset`, `from`) or, without it, the room filled with rows and aisles (`aisle`, Grid's own 6 m when left out; `rows`; `from`; `count`), `numbered` and `floor`. Bays are `<group>-<k>`, numbered on from the floor's highest number unless `numbered` gives the first; placing a group again replaces its bays. It answers with how many it placed, their ids and numbers, and what Grid chose, and `bays`, `group`, `first` and `last` in the data.
- New tool `grid_add_ramp`: `from`, `to`, `width`, then `room` with `runs` and `along` (straight), `shape` helix with `at`, `radius`, `turns`, `starts` and `clockwise`, or `run` (the middle line, points as `"x,y"`); `slope`, `name`, `id` (`ramp-<n>` when left out). It answers with the rise, the length along the middle line and the slope, and `ramp`, `rise`, `length`, `slope` and `points` in the data; a ramp that does not fit its room is refused naming the room's size. The tool server lists 55 tools; the two come after `grid_remove_item` and before `grid_set_site`.
- Rooms text: the `bay`, `bays` and `ramp` lines and `raw bay`, `raw ramp`; `rooms/bay` and `rooms/ramp` refuse a line Grid cannot lay out, with its line and what the room needs. `raw` now takes `wall`, `room`, `opening`, `floor`, `stair`, `bay` or `ramp`, and `raw stall` says where a bay goes. The answer's guesses give how many bays each line placed and each ramp's rise, length and slope. The rooms grammar has a `parking` topic and the guide a car park question. No new code in the record.
