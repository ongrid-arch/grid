# 2026-11-26: undo, restore and a change log beside the plan (CMD-44)

## For people

- Every change Grid makes to a plan now keeps the plan it replaced and a line in the plan's change log beside it (`plan.history.jsonl`, with the replaced plans in `plan.checkpoints/`): when, who, with which tool and what changed, facts and room numbers included. An agent can take the last change back with `grid_undo`, step by step, or put back any earlier version with `grid_restore`: a marker, the plan before the last change, a revision, a date and time or a commit. Neither overwrites a change made by hand or in another program without saying so. `grid diff` compares with any of those versions, and two earlier versions with each other (`--to`); `--show facts` lists every field that changed and `--show history` the log. A change of facts or numbers alone no longer reads as "Changes: none". A plan keeps its latest 20 markers, the oldest rolling off instead of a sixth being refused, and a marker keeps who set it and a note (CMD-44).

## For agents

- New tools `grid_undo` and `grid_restore`, after `grid_words` and before `grid_write_rooms` (58 tools with the annotations). `grid_undo` (`plan`, `who`) writes back the plan before the last change in the log not undone yet; data `written`, `undid` (`seq`, `at`, `by`, `tool`, `before`, `after`), `from`, `revision`, `left`, `verdict`, `changes`, `fields`. `grid_restore` (`plan`, `since` required, `force`, `who`) writes back the version `since` names; data `written`, `since`, `version` (`kind` `marker`, `git`, `revision` or `now`, `name`, `at`, `commit`, `revision`), `from`, `revision`, `forced`, `verdict`, `changes`, `fields`. Both are refused, nothing written, when the plan is not at the revision its log last wrote (`… changed outside the changes in its log …`); `grid_restore` with `force: true` restores all the same.
- Every tool that changes the plan takes `who` beside `based_on`, kept in the log's entry; every change it writes to the plan file adds one line to `<plan>.history.jsonl` (`seq`, `at`, `by`, `via`, `tool`, `before`, `after`, `verdict`, `lines`, `more`, `fields`, `fieldsMore`, and `undoes` or `restored`) and keeps the replaced text in `<plan>.checkpoints/<revision>.json` (the latest 50). `grid edit`, `grid write` and `grid tidy` log theirs too.
- `grid_diff`: `since` also takes `last`, a revision of 12 hex digits, a date (`2026-11-26`, its start) or date and time (UTC unless a zone is given), `git~N` and a commit by 7 to 40 hex digits; a marker's name is read first. New `to`, the later version, named as `since` is. `show` takes `facts` (every field that changed, `fields` in the data) and `history` (the log's entries since then, `history` in the data). Data gains `to`, `fields` (with `show: all` or `changes`, the facts and numbers only), `fieldsTotal`, `history`; `since` gains `revision`, `by` and `note`, and `kind` may be `revision` or `now`. When nothing that stands changed but fields did, the changes line is `Changes: none to what stands; <n> fields changed, <m> of them facts or numbers.`, followed by `Facts and numbers that changed (<m>):` and the count of the others (`show: facts` lists them). An unknown `since` is refused as before (`The plan has no marker "<name>": it has …`), now naming the other versions it takes.
- `grid_mark_start` takes `who` and `note`, kept with the marker; a new marker past `MARKERS_MAX` (now 20) rolls the oldest off and says `Marker "<name>", the oldest, rolled off: …`, data `rolled`. `grid_project` lists `history` and `checkpoints` beside a plan. No new code in the record.
