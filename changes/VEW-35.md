# 2026-11-25: a balcony's low walls stay low in a facade (VEW-35)

## For people

- A facade no longer draws a balcony's low walls up to the slab over them as a white box that
  hides the doors behind it; the walls round a balcony, a terrace or a fence keep their own
  height (VEW-35).

## For agents

- `grid_elevation` and the facade views: walls that bound outdoor space and no room keep their
  own top instead of rising to the slab or roof over them. No new code in the record.
