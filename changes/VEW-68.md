# 2026-11-26: room labels stay in their rooms (VEW-68)

## For people

- Plan pictures fit names and areas inside the room they describe. Repeated names carry their room numbers, and tight rooms can use a number with a key under the picture (VEW-68).

## For agents

- Plan pictures from `grid_view` lay out the labels again within a crop or tile. Room numbers stay the same across pictures of the floor. If a room has no clear place even for its number, its label waits for a closer view. No new code in the record.
