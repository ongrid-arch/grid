Grid (ongrid) and Maket

Dated 23 November 2026. Facts about Maket come from a research note read on 16 November 2026 and have not been re-checked since; check the other tool's own site for what it does today. This page describes tools; it does not rank them or say one is better.

What the research note says about Maket

  • Maket is one of the floor-plan tools that web listicles for “best AI floor plan generator 2026” keep naming, alongside others such as Planner 5D, TestFit, Finch, Snaptrude, Hypar, CubiCasa and RoomSketcher.
  • Maket publishes its own comparison article of such tools. The note observes that many such lists are written by vendors.
  • When the note was written, no app by Maket was found inside ChatGPT or Claude.

What Grid is

Grid is not a floor-plan generator website. It is an open-source toolkit an AI agent calls: the person describes a home or building, and the agent writes the plan rooms first and then measures, checks, draws and changes it with Grid's tools. The plan lives in one file you keep.

General guidance, not your local rules

Grid's checks are labelled this way in every report. No Grid message says a plan complies or is approved.

Where they may differ

Maket is a product people use through its own website; Grid is a tool for agents and works inside the assistant you already use. Beyond that, this note does not describe Maket's features, so none is compared. See Maket's own site.
