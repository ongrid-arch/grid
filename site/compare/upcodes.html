Grid (ongrid) and UpCodes

Dated 23 November 2026. Facts about UpCodes come from a research note read on 16 November 2026 and have not been re-checked since; check the other tool's own site for what it does today. This page describes tools; it does not rank them or say one is better.

What the research note says about UpCodes

  • People asking about building codes are sent to UpCodes Copilot and to ICC's AI Navigator, which the note describes as tools that look code sections up rather than check plans (a comparison by Nomic).
  • UpCodes has published a self-reported accuracy figure of 93% for its Copilot (reported by Building Enclosure). That is the vendor's own claim and Grid does not verify or repeat it as fact.

What Grid does

Grid works on a plan file: it measures the drawing and applies figures from building codes to the parts of that plan. Each figure records its document, edition, clause, where it applies, the day it was read and a status; a figure an AI found stays marked as such until a named person has checked it against the document. Today Grid applies only United States model-code figures. Grid does not copy code text and does not offer a lookup of code sections.

General guidance, not your local rules

Grid does not publish an accuracy percentage. Every report carries the label above, says what the checks found and what they did not check, and never says a plan complies or is approved. Local rules and the authority that applies to your project decide.
